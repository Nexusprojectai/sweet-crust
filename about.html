Our Story

One second-hand oven, a jar of starter, and a stubborn belief that bread should take time.

How it started

Elena baked her first loaf for a neighbour who'd just moved in down the street. Word travelled faster than the bread cooled. Within a year, she'd rented the corner unit on Mill Lane, bought a second-hand deck oven, and started feeding a sourdough starter that's still going today.

How it's going

Same corner unit. Same starter. Bigger ovens, second generation. We still mix every dough by hand, we still refuse preservatives and improvers, and we still sell out by early afternoon most Saturdays — which we take as a compliment.
